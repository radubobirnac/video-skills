# Evidence-backed creative review

Fill generated `creative-review.md` using source/corrected evidence. Mark each item `pass`, `issue`, or `unverified`; include timestamps and observations. Do not tick everything from a contact sheet. Sample around suspected problems, use a transcript for speech, and listen via available audio tools when judging sound.

| Item | Evidence to seek | Action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Opening | Frames from 0–3 s; product/hook visible | Recommend a stronger opening if a logo/blank screen delays the product; timing is a best practice |
| Sound-off comprehension | Demonstration and visible message | Flag speech-dependent messages without visible explanation |
| Text | Mobile legibility, contrast, spelling and reading time | Inspect exact text frames; do not invent product claims |
| Safe zones | Placement template and control overlaps | Use official horizontal SB template; mark other zones unverified if unavailable |
| Product relevance | Correct product, variation and ASIN | Compare supplied product information |
| Ending and pacing | Complete ending, natural cut, no blank lead/tail | Inspect adjacent frames/transcript before trimming |
| Claims | Exact wording, substantiation and category policy | Request evidence for efficacy, superiority, health and quantified claims |
| Ad restrictions | Reviews/stars, deals, Amazon branding, urgency, inappropriate content | Consult current video/category policies; apply ad rules only to ads |
| Audio and language | Audio, transcript, marketplace language and sound quality | Mark unreviewed when tools/transcript are unavailable |
| Crop and quality | Product/text after resizing, detail, bars and motion | Rescaling does not restore detail; black detection is heuristic |

Beverage footage can communicate flavor, culture and enjoyment. Do not force a medical or problem-solution story on a lifestyle product. Separate policy issues from subjective suggestions.

`--transcript` flags some claim-like phrases. It is a text heuristic, not OCR, transcription, legal analysis or a comprehensive classifier. No matches does not clear a video. Keep SRT timestamps and check matched phrases in context.

Conclude only after reviewing evidence. Use "technical checks passed; two creative changes recommended; spoken claims unverified" where appropriate. Amazon makes the moderation decision.
